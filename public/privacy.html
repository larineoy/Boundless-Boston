Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Boundless Boston (“we”, “the Service”) handles information when you use our website.

1. Information we process

  • Search inputs you submit (for example time, budget, walk limit, optional free text, and approximate location you provide for recommendations).
  • Saved places you choose to bookmark. These are stored in your browser’s local storage on your device, not in a Boundless Boston user account.
  • Optional TikTok data, only if you connect TikTok and grant permission: basic profile fields and public video metadata (such as titles/descriptions) needed to derive taste tags for personalization. Access tokens are stored server-side in a short-lived session store; they are not placed in the public response cache.
  • Technical logs needed to operate the Service (for example request health). We aim to avoid logging free text, precise coordinates, cookies, or access tokens.

2. How we use information

  • To generate and rank venue recommendations.
  • To personalize rankings when you provide preference tags from TikTok and/or saved places.
  • To operate, secure, and debug the Service.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use TikTok to discover or scrape venues. We do not post to your TikTok account.

3. Sharing

We send necessary requests to third-party providers that power recommendations (for example places, transit, weather). If you connect TikTok, authentication and authorized API calls go to TikTok under their terms. We do not share your TikTok tokens with the browser or with unrelated third parties.

4. Retention

  • Saved places remain in your browser until you remove them or clear site data.
  • TikTok sessions can be cleared with Disconnect / logout; session records are short-lived.
  • Public third-party API responses may be cached briefly for reliability, without user auth tokens.

5. Your choices

  • Use the Service without connecting TikTok.
  • Disconnect TikTok to clear the TikTok session and stop using TikTok-derived tags.
  • Remove saved places in the app or clear browser storage.

6. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: contact the Boundless Boston project maintainers via the project repository or hackathon team.
